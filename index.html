ViveLink — the on-device AI companion for your phone

A compact MagSafe device powered by large language models. ViveLink perceives your screen, reasons about what to do, and operates apps on your behalf — running multiple LLMs simultaneously while keeping your data private and on-device.

About LinChain

LinChain Co.,Ltd. (린체인 주식회사) is a South Korea-based startup developing on-device large language model technology and smart hardware. Founded in 2025 and based at the Kyonggi University Startup Incubation Center in Suwon, we are building the interface layer between people and AI — starting with ViveLink.

Supported by the Kyonggi University Startup program and the 2025 Pre-startup Package (Deep Tech).
